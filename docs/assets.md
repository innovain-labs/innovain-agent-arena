# Assets and third-party notices

All terrain, buildings, glass domes, ribs, rocks, roads, airlocks, panels, antenna, rover, dust, leak particles, and crew markers are original procedural Three.js geometry. The solar grid texture is drawn with Canvas at runtime. No external models, photographs, environment maps, sounds, or downloaded textures are included.
