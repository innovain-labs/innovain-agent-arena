# Mars Incident 003 — Critical Oxygen Shortage

Only a small shared oxygen reserve remains. Habitat B has a pressure leak and eight occupants. Primary power is healthy, and the Hub and Habitat A are pressurized.

Inspect the available reserves, pressure, and transit paths. Oxygen routing draws from the colony-wide reserve; increasing flow without containing the leak may exhaust life support. Isolation contains shared oxygen loss but closes the transit path. Choose the order of evacuation, containment, and supply carefully.

Protect every crew member, contain the leak, and stabilize Habitat B without depleting the shared reserve. Human escalation does not stop the clock.
