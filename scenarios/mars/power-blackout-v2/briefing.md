# Mars Incident 002 — Life Support Blackout

The primary grid is below the operating threshold for life support. Habitat A is losing pressure with eight occupants inside. The central Hub and Habitat B are pressurized.

Inspect colony and life-support telemetry. Backup power is available but consumes finite fuel. Oxygen routing needs operational power. Confirm a safe transit path before moving crew; isolation closes transit connections and can trap occupants.

Protect every crew member and stabilize Habitat A before the emergency deadline. Requesting human assistance records escalation but does not connect an operator or pause the incident.

## Mars operations v2 / mars-mcp-v2
