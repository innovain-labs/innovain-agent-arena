# SOL 183 / Mars Colony Alpha

A pressure leak has been detected in Habitat B. Eight crew members are inside. Pressure is falling, and the colony has limited oxygen reserves. Life support and primary power are operational.

Protect all crew and stabilize Habitat B. Inspect telemetry before choosing an intervention. Transit requires at least 50 kPa along an open connection. Isolation closes transit connections but does not repair a leak. Rerouting oxygen draws from the shared reserve. External airlocks vent directly into the Martian atmosphere.

You control the colony through MCP tools. Tool calls take simulation time. The simulator determines every consequence. Human assistance can be requested, but no operator is connected in the example session.

## Mars operations v2 / mars-mcp-v2

Protect the crew, repair the leak, and restore Habitat B pressure. The repair drone needs isolation, an evacuated habitat, and operating power. Repair seals a leak but does not refill pressure or stop an existing oxygen flow. Inspect tool descriptions for prerequisites. Emergency conservation reduces baseline oxygen use by 75%, but does not reduce leak losses or supplied oxygen. Power rerouting adds up to 20 units once by disconnecting nonessential services. These actions consume simulation time.
